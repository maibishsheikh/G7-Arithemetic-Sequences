# Story Art Brief — ProgressionQuest (Grade 7 · Arithmetic Sequences)

This directory contains the visual story assets for ProgressionQuest.

## Story Panels Specification

### Panel 1: Signal Lost (`1.png`)
- **Scene:** Mission Control room, main telemetry console.
- **Characters:** Ishaan (meticulous South Asian cadet) and Xin Yi (confident East Asian cadet) on shift.
- **Key Action:** Flashing amber telemetry alert on screen showing checkpoint readings 1 to 5, with reading 6 corrupted by cosmic interference.
- **Emotion:** Alert, curious, urgent.

### Panel 2: Naming the Pattern (`2.png`)
- **Scene:** Mission Control console holographic display.
- **Characters:** Orbit the Mission Bot hovering between Ishaan and Xin Yi.
- **Key Action:** Orbit projecting holographic arithmetic sequence notation: First term (`a`) and common difference (`d`), actively flagging that Xin Yi only checked the first gap and reminding cadets to check *every* consecutive gap.
- **Emotion:** Instructive, friendly, enlightening.

### Panel 3: The Trajectory Formula (`3.png`)
- **Scene:** Mission Control flight calculator observatory.
- **Characters:** Ishaan pointing to holographic general term formula: `Tn = a + (n - 1)d`, Orbit demonstrating a descending rocket stage with negative difference `d`. Xin Yi taking notes on flight pad.
- **Key Action:** Visual demonstration that `d` can be negative during descent and that `(n - 1)` groups of `d` are added to `a`.
- **Emotion:** Focused, mathematical discovery.

### Panel 4: Reading Six, Recovered (`4.png`)
- **Scene:** Mission Control main flight screen celebration.
- **Characters:** Ishaan and Xin Yi high-fiving, Orbit the Mission Bot flashing celebratory green sparks.
- **Key Action:** Giant screen shows: "Checkpoint 6 Confirmed: 54m — Flight Path Restored!", Rocket Nova-7 successfully docking in orbit.
- **Emotion:** Triumphant, accomplished, energized.
